Privacy policy

ebaugh.net personal and household apps · Effective November 23, 2026

The apps on ebaugh.net, including Route Exporter and small household tools that sign in with Google, are run by one household for its own use. They are not a commercial service. They show no ads, and they do not sell, rent or share anyone's information.

Apps that sign in with Google

Some household tools ask a household member to sign in with Google so they can read and write files in that person's own Google account, for example to keep backups in the person's own Google Drive. Those tools:

  • use the access only for the task the person set up, on the household's own computers;
  • keep the data in the signed-in person's own Google account and send it nowhere else;
  • do not use Google user data to build profiles, advertise, or train AI models, and do not transfer it to anyone else.

Their use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can remove an app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Route Exporter

Route Exporter has no accounts. Trips saved in it are stored by the site's host (Netlify) and are open: anyone with the site can view, change or delete them. Do not put private information in a saved trip. To draw a route, the leg's start and end points are sent to Google's routing service. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap, which see the map areas you view.

Hosting and logs

The site's host keeps routine technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for operating and protecting the site. The household does not use them to identify or track visitors.
